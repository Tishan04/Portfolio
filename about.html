How I think

Principles I try to bring into every project.

These can grow with the portfolio. Each card is intentionally independent, so you can duplicate, remove, or rewrite it without changing the layout.

01

Understand first

Clarify what the user needs, what the system must do, and where the real constraints are before choosing an implementation.

02

Keep it clear

Prefer understandable structures, interfaces, naming, and flows over complexity that does not improve the outcome.

03

Build, test, improve

Get the important functionality working, test it carefully, then refine the details that make the final product feel polished.
